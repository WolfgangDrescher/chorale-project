A group query runs a query and groups its matches by features of the score, e.g. by the interval between bass and soprano and each interval by the metric weight of the bass note. Every group carries the query that finds exactly its matches, and variants such as counting the intervals without their quality can be switched on the page. The tables of the lessons are built the same way.
