While a chorale plays, the score follows along, a click on a note starts playback from there, and the chords can be stepped through one at a time. The key a note is in comes from the annotated modulations, and so far only a few chorales have them. That is why anyone can now annotate a chorale while listening to it: download the annotations as JSON or send them in as a GitHub issue, and once they are added they reach the timeline and the search. The lyrics cover 353 of the 370 chorales, and a search finds words, syllables and melismas.
