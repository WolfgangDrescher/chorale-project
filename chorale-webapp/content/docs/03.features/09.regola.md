The rule of the octave sets a chord over each scale degree of the bass, over
some scale degrees depending on how the bass goes on: up by step, down by step,
or by leap. Up and down the scales of C major and C minor:

| Key               | Value             | Driving feature |
|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| `regolaChordTone` | `true` or `false` | no              |
| `regolaChord`     | `true` or `false` | no              |

Both take the chord of a position from the bass that sounds under it, its scale
degree and where it goes on: up or down by step or by leap. `regolaChordTone`
asks whether the note of the voice the query searches is a tone of that chord,
`regolaChord` whether all notes at the position are.



## `regolaChordTone`: one note

`regolaChordTone` asks of the note a position of the match finds whether it is a
tone of the chord over the bass at its onset. Only the chords of
[`regolaTags`](/docs/options#regolatags) count, `core` if it is left out.

The notes of the soprano over the seventh scale degree of the bass that are no
tone of its chord:

```json
{
    "feature": "kern",
    "voices": "soprano",
    "regolaTags": ["core"],
    "pattern": [
        { "@1": { "deg": ["7", "7+"] }, "regolaChordTone": false }
    ]
}
```

Driven by `kern`, a position is a note the voice attacks, and its chord is the
one at the attack. Driven by
[`fb`](/docs/features/fb#fb-as-the-driving-feature), every line of the `**kern`
score is a position, so a held note meets each chord it is held into.



## `regolaChord`: the whole sonority

`regolaChord` checks everything the voices above the bass sound at a position,
a held note included, and is `true` when each of them is a tone of its chord.
It reads the moment at which the position begins, whatever voice and feature
the query searches. The bass going from :deg{v="4" bass} to :deg{v="3" bass}
with a chord of the rule of the octave over both:

```json
{
    "feature": "kern",
    "voices": "bass",
    "regolaTags": ["core"],
    "pattern": [
        { "deg": "4", "regolaChord": true },
        { "deg": "3", "regolaChord": true }
    ]
}
```



## How the chord is chosen

Every line of the `**kern` score gets a chord of its own, so a note is always
measured over the bass that sounds with it.

1. **Bass:** the note the bass sounds on the line, attacked there or held. A
   line on which the bass rests has no chord.
2. **Bass motion:** whether the bass goes on up or down by step or by leap. Over
   some scale degrees it decides the chord: :deg{v="7" bass} takes :fb{v="6 5"}
   going up and :fb{v="6"} going down. A step counts to the next note of the
   bass on another scale degree, or to the next such note with a [metric
   weight](/docs/features/metweight) (`strong`, `half-strong` or `weak`), so
   that the notes of a diminution in between do not count. A note the bass
   repeats or only alters is passed over.
3. **Scale degree:** the scale degree of the bass in the local key, as
   [`deg`](/docs/features/deg) writes it. In minor the sixth and the seventh
   scale degree of the bass follow melodic minor: going up they are raised,
   :deg{v="6+ 7+ 1" bass}, going down they come from natural minor,
   :deg{v="1 7 6 5" bass}. The raised fourth scale degree, :deg{v="4+" bass},
   has chords of its own. In lydian the fourth is read as in major: the perfect
   fourth as :deg{v="4" bass}, the fourth of the mode as :deg{v="4+" bass}.
4. **Chord:** of the chords of [`regolaTags`](/docs/options#regolatags) that fit
   scale degree, bass motion and mode, a few being for major or for minor only,
   the one that explains the most notes above the bass on the line, a held note
   included. A chord may be incomplete, a :fb{v="6 5"} contains a :fb{v="6"}. On
   a tie a chord of `core` wins over one of `extended`, a plain chord over an
   enriched one. A line on which no chord explains a note has none.

A note of a diminution in the bass gets a chord of its own as well, or none
where no chord explains the notes above it, as often at a passing note.



## Qualities of the figures

A figure counts the interval above the bass. Its quality, perfect, major, minor,
augmented or diminished, follows from the local key, as in a figured bass, and
from an accidental of the figure. In minor the sixth and the seventh scale
degree may be natural or raised. `semitonium-modi` makes the seventh scale
degree the semitone below the tonic, `major-sixth` the sixth above the bass
major. Every figure is a tone of its chord, a dissonance included, as the
*quinta syncopata* or the suspended 4 of :fb{v="5 4"} over :deg{v="5" bass}; a
chromatic passing note is none.

A church mode counts as major or minor by its third; phrygian, locrian and an
undefined key have no chord.

`regolaChordTone` holds neither `true` nor `false` for the bass itself, and
neither key at a rest or on a line without a chord, so a negated key does not
find these either.



## Tags

The chords follow [Kartimento](https://glarean.mh-freiburg.de/kartimento/),
which adds further contexts to the rule of the octave: a bass that leaps,
suspensions, chords of affect. Each chord carries tags, by which
[`regolaTags`](/docs/options#regolatags) chooses the chords a query allows,
`["core"]` if it is left out.

`core` and `extended` divide all chords between them:

| Tag        | Chords                                                              |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `core`     | the context and chords of the rule of the octave, red in Kartimento |
| `extended` | the further contexts                                                |

Every other tag marks some of these chords, to add them or leave them out:
`["core", "!enriched"]` is the rule of the octave without its enriched chords,
`["core", "leap"]` adds the chords for a bass that leaps.

| Tag                 | Chords                                                                          | In                 |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| `enriched`          | the sixth chords enriched with a fourth, :fb{v="4 3"}                           | `core`             |
| `quinta-syncopata`  | :fb{v="6 5"} over :deg{v="4" bass} going up, its fifth a syncopated dissonance  | `core`             |
| `major-sixth`       | the chords with a major sixth over :deg{v="6" bass} going down                  | `core`             |
| `semitonium-modi`   | the chords that make the seventh scale degree the semitone below the tonic      | `core`, `extended` |
| `passing`           | :fb{v="6 4 2"} over :deg{v="4" bass} going down and the passing six-four chords | `core`, `extended` |
| `leap`              | the chords for a bass that leaps                                                | `extended`         |
| `affect`            | the affect chords, as the augmented sixth chords and the Neapolitan sixth       | `extended`         |
| `suspension`        | :fb{v="5 4"} and :fb{v="6 4"} over :deg{v="5" bass}, suspended                  | `extended`         |
| `double-suspension` | :fb{v="6 4"} over :deg{v="5" bass} with both suspended                          | `extended`         |
